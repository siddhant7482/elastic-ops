Silences

WhatReasonMuted byExpires
Every silence expires — the form will not accept one longer than a week. A mute with no end date is how monitoring quietly dies: the thing stays quiet long after the reason is gone and nobody remembers it was muted. Silencing stops the message, never the measurement; state changes are still recorded and still appear on the timeline.
